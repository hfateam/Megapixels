A teacher-written note at the end of every course

Parents already value Megapixels feedback more than anything else. A short end-of-course note from the teacher turns that into the reason to come back.

"When Emily started, she struggled with proportions. Over the past 12 weeks, she learned to observe details, shade confidently, and persevere through difficult drawings. One thing I especially noticed was how much her confidence grew. I think she'd thrive in our Animal Artist track next."

Send it in the celebration email with the certificate and a before and after of their artwork, and one build covers four of the top five ideas.
